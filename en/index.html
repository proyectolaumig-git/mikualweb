FAQ

What people ask before we start.

Why does the diagnosis have a cost?

Because it is real work: we study how your business operates, its processes and its data, and deliver a map of prioritized opportunities with estimated impact. A small, fixed-price investment so you don’t get the big one wrong.

What happens after the diagnosis?

You decide. With the map in hand, we prepare a proposal in 5 to 10 business days and present it to you in a session. You can continue with us, do it with your own team, or do nothing if it isn’t worth it.

Do you use artificial intelligence?

Only when it is the best answer to the problem. Often a simple automation or a well-built dashboard does more than an AI model.

Do you work with businesses outside Mexico City?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses across Mexico.

Do I need to know about technology?

No. You know your business; we translate the technical side into business decisions, in plain language.
