MIKUAL Method

We question. We choose. We build.

Our motto isn’t a slogan: it’s the method. We follow it on every project, in this order, and each stage has a result you can see.

01

We question

We study the business, its processes and its data before proposing anything.

  • We understand how your business runs today.
  • We review available processes and data.
  • We define which number should move.
You getClarity on the real problem.
02

We choose

We prioritize by impact and effort with business judgment, even if the answer is “you don’t need this”.

  • We map the opportunities.
  • We rank them by impact vs. effort.
  • We drop what isn’t worth it.
You getA prioritized plan.
03

We build

We build what was chosen and measure it against the agreed metric.

  • Automation, digital product or data solution.
  • A build scoped to what was chosen.
  • Measured against the agreed metric.
You getA working solution.
Why this order

The question first, then the technology.

The industry habit is to answer “what do you want me to build?” instead of “what’s going on in your business?”. That’s how you end up with the system nobody uses, the app that didn’t move sales, and the license you keep paying for out of habit.

We use AI only when it’s the best answer to the problem, not because it’s trendy.

How we work with you

Each step lowers the risk of the next.

It all starts with an introduction call. Then you move forward only if it’s worth it.
