How it works

You complete a questionnaire (1 to 3 hours) and we study your business, its processes and its data.

02

We choose

We prioritize by impact and effort with business judgment.

→

The prioritized map and the recommended next step: the Proposal. You decide whether we continue.

Is it for you?

It’s for you if…

  • Your business grew and the usual processes no longer keep up.
  • A vendor quoted you an expensive build and you want a second opinion.
  • You already invested in a tool that didn’t work and don’t want to repeat the mistake.
  • You have a product idea and don’t know where to start.
Useful honesty

You may not need it if…

  • You already know exactly what to build, with scope and metric defined.
  • You’re looking for someone to code a list of requirements without questioning it.

If that’s you, write to us anyway and we’ll tell you whether it makes sense to work together.

How to start

Three steps.

  1. 01

    Book a call

    An introduction and discovery call: we get to know each other, listen to your needs and propose the Diagnosis.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis

    You complete a questionnaire (1 to 3 hours) and over 5 business days we question and choose with you.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    We prepare it in 5 to 10 business days and present it to you in a session.

What do I get at the end?

A map of prioritized opportunities with estimated impact and the recommended next step.

Why does the diagnosis have a cost?

Because it is real work: we study how your business operates, its processes and its data, and deliver a map of prioritized opportunities with estimated impact. A small, fixed-price investment so you don’t get the big one wrong.

What happens after the diagnosis?

You decide. With the map in hand, we prepare a proposal in 5 to 10 business days and present it to you in a session. You can continue with us, do it with your own team, or do nothing if it isn’t worth it.

How much of my team’s time does it take?

1 to 3 hours to complete the Diagnosis questionnaire.
